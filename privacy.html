Privacy Policy

Applies to the "GTA VI Engine" app. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

What this app is

GTA VI Engine is a private publishing tool used by one person, the owner of the connected Facebook Page and YouTube channels, to upload and schedule their own videos and to read the performance statistics of those videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

  • Access tokens for the owner's own Facebook Page and YouTube channels, stored only on the owner's computer.
  • Statistics for the owner's own posts and videos (for example views, watch time and follower counts), stored only on the owner's computer.

AI processing

To write scripts and weekly performance reports for the owner, the app sends the text of the owner's own scripts and summary statistics of the owner's own videos to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude). No information about viewers, commenters, followers or any other person is included.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data about any other person, including viewers, commenters or followers.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, other than the AI processing described above.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Data retention and deletion

All data stays on the owner's computer. Removing the app from the Facebook Page settings or the Google account permissions page revokes its access, and deleting the app's local folder deletes all stored data. To request deletion of anything related to this app, contact the Page owner through the Facebook Page's messages.
