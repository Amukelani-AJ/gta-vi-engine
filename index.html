GTA VI Engine

A private publishing tool for one creator's own YouTube channels and Facebook Page.

GTA VI Engine helps its owner edit, schedule and publish their own gaming videos about Grand Theft Auto VI, and read the performance statistics of those videos. It runs on the owner's own computer and has no other users. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

It uses the YouTube Data API, the YouTube Analytics API and the Facebook Pages API only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to read statistics about them.
